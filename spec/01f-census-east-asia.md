# 01f. Census: East and Southeast Asia

Status: research note. Date: 2026-11-16. Scope: the competitions of China, Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, Japan, Korea (South and North), Mongolia, Vietnam, Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar and Brunei, at every school level, and the places that publish their problems. This note is phase 1 of the census. Nothing was harvested. A few sample pages were read to confirm the shape of a paper.

## 0. How to read this note

- **[P]** marks a primary source: the organizer's own site, file or book.
- **[S]** marks a secondary source: a mirror, a forum, a blog, a dataset or a third-party archive.
- The robots.txt files and terms were read on 2026-11-16. "Robots" gives what the file says for `User-agent: *` and for named AI crawlers.
- A zone follows spec 11: Z2 means that the content may hold the statement with a link to the source; Z3 means that the content holds a link only.
- A competition with no layout or era counts no expected positions. The census does not guess positions.

Key findings:

- The register had 25 codes for the region. The census adds 88 codes: 60 for high school, 16 for junior, 10 for elementary and 2 for undergraduates. Nine new codes have a shape from a primary source and add 1,399 expected positions (section 1.1). `kr-kmo` gets a first year and eras from the KMO rules and now expects 236 positions. The other 79 new codes have no shape, and each description says why.
- Most lower rounds had no code, so some aliases sent them to final-round codes: the PMO qualifying and area stages, the PMO orals, the JMO and JJMO preliminaries, the Singapore junior and senior sections and the HRM8K first-round rows of KMO and KJMO. Each now has its own code.
- Official archives are rare. Japan (imojp) and Hong Kong (EDB) forbid reuse. Taiwan (TMO working group), Malaysia (IMO Malaysia), Mongolia (MMO committee), Thailand (POSN) and the Philippines (pmo.ph) publish papers with a copyright line and no licence. China, Korea, Singapore, Vietnam and Indonesia have no usable official archive.
- Several sites in the region allow all agents but block named AI crawlers through the Cloudflare managed robots.txt, with `ai-train=no`: pmo.ph, mathcenter.net and AoPS. ipst.ac.th also blocks ClaudeBot. The owner has not decided the rule for these sites, so the decision is pending.

- No official paper or rules found: most Chinese codes, `hk-chkmo`, `tw-imoc`, `jp-egmo-prelim`, `kr-uscm`, `kp-mo`, `kp-tst`, the TSTs of Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand, `my-jom`, `th-tomo`, `th-mathcenter`, `th-t3mo`, `vn-vmeo`, `sg-smops`.

- Papers that share problem numbers: `my-imonst1`, `my-imonst2`, `vn-homc`, `vn-olympic-304`, `cn-cmc`, `sg-smo-junior`.

## 5. Pull requests

The census went to tamnd/olymmath in three register PRs, one for each country group. Each was rebuilt on the main of its day, passed tcheck with "non-warning 0" and the slim register check, and was squash-merged.

- tamnd/olymmath#256: Greater China, 22 codes, 162 expected positions, 5 sources, 17 no-source gaps, 148 alias lines.
- tamnd/olymmath#257: Japan, Korea and Mongolia, 15 codes, 1,237 expected positions (and `kr-kmo` 236), 2 sources, 6 no-source gaps, 35 alias lines.
- tamnd/olymmath#258: Southeast Asia, 51 codes, no expected position, 6 sources, 12 no-source gaps, 1 robots gap (`ph-mtap`), 78 alias lines.

No full-content check, audit or coverage run was made for these PRs. The coverage numbers will move at the next coverage run.
