How olymmath works

Human text first

The statement of each problem comes from the official paper or a human collection. A model never writes a statement.

Each text has a source

Each statement and each solution shows its source, its origin and its licence zone, with a link to the original.

AI with a label

AI solutions come after the human solutions. Each one shows its model, its grade and who graded it.
