About olymmath

olymmath collects the problems and the solutions of all mathematical olympiads in one place. It shows the human text first, and it shows the source of each text.

The rules

  1. One problem has one page. When a problem appears in two competitions, for example in the IMO Shortlist and in the IMO, the page shows the two appearances.
  2. The statement of a problem comes from a human source. The order of preference is: the official text, an official translation, a curated collection, a community post, a dataset. A model never writes a statement.
  3. Each text shows its source, its origin and its licence zone.
  4. Official and human solutions come first. AI solutions come after them, with the model, the date and the grade.
  5. olymmath does not go around the bot protection of any site. When a site does not allow copies, olymmath shows a link.

Difficulty

A difficulty band goes from 1, the easiest, to 10, the hardest. The dots show the band in five steps. For now, the band comes only from the position of the problem in its paper, as in spec 4.9 of the toolchain:

  • IMO: problems 1 and 4 are band 6, problems 2 and 5 are band 7, and problems 3 and 6 are band 9.
  • USAMO: problems 1 and 4 are band 6, problems 2 and 5 are band 7, and problems 3 and 6 are band 8.
  • IMO Shortlist: in each topic, problems 1 and 2 are band 5, 3 and 4 are band 6, 5 and 6 are band 7, 7 and 8 are band 8, and the problems after them are band 9.

Other competitions have no band yet. A problem that is in the IMO and in the IMO Shortlist takes the band of its primary appearance.

Licence zones

Z1, open licence
The full text is on olymmath, under its licence, with credit to the author.
Z2, quoted with source
The statement and the facts are on olymmath, with the source and a link. A solution shows its author, a short excerpt and a link.
Z3, link only
olymmath shows the facts and a link to the source.

Licences of olymmath

The metadata of olymmath is under CC0 1.0. The solutions of contributors are under CC BY-SA 4.0. The code is under the Apache License 2.0. A third-party text keeps its own licence. Each page tells you which licence applies.

Remove a text

Are you the holder of the rights of a text on olymmath? Open an issue in the content repository, and we remove the text or change it to a link in 7 days.

The project

The content is in tamnd/olymmath. The toolchain is in tamnd/olymmath-agent. The specification is in the spec directory of the toolchain.

Search

Type words to search the statements.

Words find the problems that have all of them. A query with a TeX command, a script, a brace or an equals sign is a formula: x^2+y^2 finds the statements with that formula, and \frac12 is the same as \frac{1}{2}. A locator such as imo/2024/p1 or a tag goes to its page when you press Enter.

    Coverage

    The register gives the expected positions of each competition. These measures tell how many of them olymmath has, and how good the texts are.

    C0 is the share of expected positions with a problem. C1 is the share with a canonical statement written by a person (class H1 or H2). C2 is the share with an official text. C3 is the share with a verified statement. C4 is the share with a human or official solution. C5 to C8 count graded solutions, checked answers, formal statements and formal proofs. olymmath does not record these yet, so they show a dash. When the register gives no positions for a competition, the problems that olymmath has are the expected positions, and C0 shows a dash.
